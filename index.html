Our purpose

A kinder world begins with care.

The Couton Foundation is committed to animal welfare and environmental protection — because the well-being of animals, people, and the planet are deeply connected.

Our areas of interest span domestic animals, wildlife, and endangered species, alongside the conservation of natural habitats, oceans, waterways, and forests. We also support greater awareness of pollution and climate-related challenges.

We believe meaningful progress grows through compassion, responsible stewardship, and collaboration.

What we care about

One mission. Many ways to make a difference.

Our broad mission embraces animals and the natural systems that sustain life.

Domestic Animal Welfare

Supporting compassion for companion animals, including dogs and cats, and the shelters and rescue efforts that care for them.

Wildlife & Endangered Species

Recognizing the importance of protecting wildlife, biodiversity, and the habitats animals need to thrive.

Environmental Protection

Promoting care for forests, oceans, waterways, natural habitats, and a cleaner, more sustainable environment.

Our approach

Protecting what matters, together.

We aim to encourage practical action, build awareness, and support efforts that make a positive difference for animals and the environment.
